# Futsal booking FAQ

## Opening hours
Our courts are open Sunday to Friday from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. On Saturdays we open at 7:00 AM and close at 11:00 PM.

## Courts and locations
We have 5-a-side and 7-a-side courts at our Baneshwor venue. Both court types have artificial turf and floodlights for evening games.

## Prices
5-a-side court: Rs 1,800 per hour. 7-a-side court: Rs 2,500 per hour. Evening slots between 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM cost Rs 500 extra per hour. Bookings are charged per hour, so a 2-hour booking costs twice the hourly price.

## Payment methods
You can pay with eSewa, Khalti, or cash at the venue. Online payments confirm your booking automatically within a few minutes.

## Changing a booking
You can reschedule a booking free of charge up to 6 hours before the start time, subject to availability. Contact support with your booking ID to request a change.

## Footwear and equipment
Please wear non-marking futsal or sports shoes. Metal studs are not allowed on the turf.

## Discounts
We don't currently offer discounts. Our prices are listed on the booking page.
